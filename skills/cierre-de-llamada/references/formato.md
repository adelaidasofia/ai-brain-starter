# Output format

Seven blocks, this order, these headings. Translate the headings into the language of the call; keep the order.

Any fact not in the transcript → `[MISSING: what to ask]`.

---

## 1. SUMMARY

5 lines max. Pastes into a CRM without editing.

```
Client: <company> / <person and title>
Date: <call date>
They want: <need, in their words>
Today they use: <what they have now / with whom>
We agreed: <the concrete commitment>
```

## 2. DEAL STATE

```
State: Closed | Not closed | In follow-up
Amount: <figure mentioned, or [MISSING: price never discussed]>
Signs off: <who approves, or [MISSING: ask who signs]>
Main objection: "<verbatim client quote>"
Temperature: High | Medium | Low. <one line of why>
```

Temperature is justified by something the client said, never by intuition.

## 3. TASKS

Only commitments someone actually made on the call. No invented tasks.

```
- [ ] <action> | <owner> | <date> <time>
```

**Markdown checkbox, never a table.** The block has to paste into a task app and stay tickable. A table looks good and does nothing.

No date agreed → `[MISSING: agree a date]`. Do not supply a "reasonable" one.

## 4. FOLLOW-UP EMAIL

Send-ready draft, in the client's language.

```
Subject: <subject>

<body>
```

Body rules:
- 120 words max.
- Open with something the client said, quoted. Not "great speaking with you".
- Exactly one ask, at the end, concrete, with a date.
- No exclamation marks. No "hope you're doing well".
- Nothing that was not discussed on the call.

## 5. REMINDER

```
Next touch: <date> at <time>
Channel: <call | email | WhatsApp, whichever was agreed>
Purpose: <what happens on that touch>
```

Nothing agreed → `[MISSING: no next step was set. This is the first thing to fix.]`

## 6. NOTE TO THE SALES LEAD

One paragraph, 4 lines max. What a manager needs so they do not have to listen to the call.

Include: who, what they want, where it landed, and what the rep needs (if anything).

## 7. WHAT WORKED / WHAT DID NOT

Two lists, 3 points max each. Every point is a concrete moment with its quote.

```
Worked:
- <moment>: "<quote>"

Did not work:
- <moment>: "<quote>"
```

If the call went well and nothing belongs under "did not work", write `- Nothing evident on this call.` Do not invent a criticism to fill space.

This block is what turns a new rep into one with context. It is the most valuable and the most skipped. Do not skip it.
